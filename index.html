THE BEGINNING OF A NEW ERA

Two-inch quadruplex videotape

In 1956 Ampex demonstrated the VRX-1000 at a broadcasters’ convention in Chicago. The machine recorded a television signal on magnetic tape. Four rotating video heads swept across the moving tape, giving the heads the writing speed needed to preserve the picture. The word quadruplex refers to those four heads.

Broadcasters could replay programs hours after transmission. NBC used two-inch quad recordings of The Tonight Show for its West Coast broadcast. Tape could be reused, and many early programs were overwritten.

In this studio the dark ribbon represents oxide-coated magnetic videotape. The lighting emphasizes its edge and wrap around the guides. The threading and controls are an interpretive reconstruction.
